## Proposed educational and creative studies

- Finnish poetry: compare explicitly annotated verses, record disagreements
  and keep orthographic measures distinct from phonetic or metrical analyses.
- Artwork: compare complete sampled color histograms and independently
  reviewed perceptual color measures; use synthetic known-answer fixtures.
- Music: distinguish measured acoustic statistics from symbolic score structure.
- Mathematical aesthetics: circular rhythm geometry and regular polygons.
  v7.9 implements a bounded floor-difference pattern, not an audio analyzer.
- Research visualization: show numeric results, provenance and limitations
  together, without collapsing unrelated measurements into one quality score.

Do not copy external code, datasets or model weights without reviewing their
licenses and actual suitability. Android availability has not been verified.
